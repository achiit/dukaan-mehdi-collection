In my own words

The name 313 comes from a number that stayed with me since childhood: a small group that stood together and did more than anyone expected. That is the spirit I want for this brand.

My elder cousin once told me, sitting on a cold winter evening in Athoora, that opportunity in Kashmir rarely walks to your door and you have to build it together. I never forgot that. We are at the start of the journey now, and every product and service here is being made carefully, not rushed.

If you have an idea, a question or a business you want to bring online, message me directly. I read every message myself.
